1. Install and sign in

  1. Scan the Master Bill QR code with the phone camera and open the link.
  2. Download master-bill.apk and tap it. If Android asks, allow “Install unknown apps” for the browser.
  3. Open Master Bill. The Techyverve start screen shows for a moment.
  4. Type your Login ID (for example ramesh) or the owner’s email, and the password the owner gave you. Tap Sign In.
You stay signed in until you tap Sign Out (Settings, at the bottom). Closing the app, restarting the phone or losing internet does not sign you out.
New versions use the same QR code. Installing over the old app keeps everyone signed in.

3. Tables and taking an order

The orange card shows money on tables now, and how many tables are running, free, and parcels.

  • Free (white): tap to start an order.
  • Eating (orange): order running, with amount and time.
  • Billed (purple): bill printed, waiting for payment.

Pull the screen down to refresh. Orders from other phones appear by themselves.

  1. Tap ADD on a dish; use − and + to change the quantity.
  2. Use the category chips or the search box to find dishes. ★ dishes show first.
  3. The dark bar at the bottom shows items and total. Tap View Order.
  4. Tap Save Order. Taps are kept on the phone until saved, so nothing is lost if the internet drops.
Leaving the screen saves the order automatically.

4. Sections: two groups at one table

When two different groups sit at the same table, each can have its own bill.

  1. Open the table (it must already have dishes) and tap ＋ Section at the top right.
  2. Section B opens. Take the second group’s order.
  3. Switch with the Section A / Section B tabs, each showing its total. Each section is billed separately.

5. Voice search

  1. Tap the mic next to the search box.
  2. Say the dish, for example “chicken biryani” or “puneri misal”.
  3. The best matching dishes appear. Tap ADD.

It understands short or Indian spellings: “chin biryani” finds Chicken Biryani, “pav baji” finds Pav Bhaji. Turn it on or off in Settings → Printer & this phone → Voice search.

6. Print the bill and take payment

  1. Open the order and tap View Order.
  2. Enter a discount if needed.
  3. Under the total, choose how the customer pays: Cash, UPI, Card or Split.
  4. Tap Print Bill. The bill prints with the payment and the table becomes free.
If the owner turned off “Print Bill clears the table”, the table waits as Billed. Open it and tap Receive Payment when the customer pays.

Split payment

  1. Tap Split.
  2. Type the first amount, for example Cash 400.
  3. Tap the next box: the rest fills in by itself (UPI 272).
  4. When it says ✓ Adds up to the total, tap Print Bill. Each part is printed under the total.

7. Parcels and kitchen tickets

Parcels

  1. On Tables, tap Parcel (bag icon), add dishes and tap Print Bill.
  2. With a kitchen printer set up: the kitchen copy prints there by itself.
  3. Without one: the customer’s bill prints first; tear it off, then tap Print for the kitchen copy on the same printer (or Skip).

Kitchen tickets (KOT) for tables

Only when the owner turns on Kitchen tickets (KOT) for tables in Settings. Then the order sheet shows Send to Kitchen: it prints only the dishes added or cancelled since the last ticket, with the table in big letters. Without this setting there is no kitchen ticket for tables.

8. Bills (Settings → Bills)

  • The card shows the day’s sales and the Cash / UPI / Card split. Tap Today to pick another date.
  • Waiting for payment: bills printed but not paid.
  • Tap a bill to see it and Print it again.

Owner only

  • Edit Bill: change quantities, add a dish, discount or payment. Saved in the change log.
  • Delete: type the confirmation word that Techyverve gave the owner, then tap Delete Bill. Keep this word private.

9. Analytics (Settings → Analytics)

  • Choose Today, Yesterday, 7 days, 30 days, This month or Last month.
  • See total sales and the change from the period before, bills, average bill, dishes sold, discount, GST, parcels, average time at table, busiest hour and best day.
  • Charts: sales by day, busy hours, payments, weekdays; then top dishes and categories.
  • Tap the download button on the orange card to share a spreadsheet (WhatsApp, email, Drive).
Analytics loads only when you open it or change the dates. Pull down to refresh.

10. Menu (owner)

  • The switch marks a dish sold out for today; tap it again when it is back.
  • Tap ★ to show a dish first when taking orders.
  • Tap a dish to edit its name, price, category or veg/non-veg, or to delete it.
  • Tap ＋ Add Item for a new dish. Choose a category or tap ＋ New.

Tables already running keep the old price; new orders use the new one. Other phones get menu changes when they next open the app.

11. Settings

  • Printer & this phone: Printer Setup and Voice search.
  • Your profile and the hotel’s details.
  • Bills and Analytics.
  • Owner: bill details (phone, GST, “Print Bill clears the table”, kitchen tickets), tables, rooms and staff logins.
  • Sign Out at the bottom.

Printer Setup

  • Bill printer: tap Bluetooth and tap your printer in the list, or tap Wi-Fi and type its IP address (on the printer’s self-test slip), then Test & Save.
  • Separate kitchen printer: switch on and choose it the same way (Bluetooth or Wi-Fi).
  • Choose paper 58 mm or 80 mm and the blank space after each slip, then Print Test.

Staff logins (owner)

  • ＋ Add Staff Login: name, password and role (Waiter or Manager). Share the Login ID and password shown.
  • To Manager / To Waiter changes the role. Password sets a new one. Remove stops that person signing in.
  • The number of logins depends on your plan. Contact Techyverve for more.

Tables (owner)

Tap a table to rename it, long-press to remove it, ＋ Add Table for a new one.

12. Rooms (lodge plan)

  • Rooms tab: tap a free room to Check In (guest, phone, guests, dates, rate).
  • Open a room to change dates, rate or discount, add extra charges (breakfast, laundry), Print the room bill, Check Out with Cash/UPI/Card, or cancel the check-in.
  • The owner adds rooms in Settings → Rooms.

13. Questions and fixes

“No access yet”Ask the owner to add you in Settings → Staff Logins.
“Service paused”Contact Techyverve. Your bills and menu are safe.
